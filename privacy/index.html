Privacy

Triangle Tots is published by Danford Enterprises, LLC. Here is everything this site keeps, and it isn't much.

Daily visit counts

Each day we count page views by site, page path and the referring website's hostname. We count taps on a few buttons the same way, by the button's name and nothing more, and a joined list is counted as a tap. That's a tally, not a record of anyone.

  • No cookie, no IP address stored, no user agent and no per-visitor id.
  • Your address is held in memory for up to an hour, only to limit abuse, and is never written to disk.
  • Daily counts are kept for 400 days.

The email lists

If you join the Sunday plan or the family waitlist, we keep your email address, which list you joined, the area if you give one, and the date.

  • It's kept apart from Tot Routines family data and never linked to it.
  • It's never sold, and never shared except with the sending service that delivers the email.
  • It stays until you leave. Every email has a link to leave, it takes one click, and leaving deletes the entry.

On your phone

The age, nap, wake-up time and area you pick are saved in your own browser so the site opens where you left it. They aren't sent to us. This site sets no cookies and has no ads, no trackers and no third-party scripts. Its fonts and maps come from this site too.

Other sites

The site itself is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server records as any web host does. Links to an event's page or to Google Maps take you to those sites, and their own policies apply there.
