About Triangle Tots

One real outing a day for your child’s age, and the whole day fitted around the nap. Free, with no ads, for families around Raleigh, Durham and Cary.

Why it’s here

Finding something to do with a toddler isn’t hard. The Triangle’s libraries, parks and museums list hundreds of things every week. The hard part is the rest: which one suits a 2-year-old, whether you’ll be home before the nap, what it costs, whether you had to sign up last Tuesday. Triangle Tots does that part. It picks one outing and plans the day around it, so the answer to “what are we doing today?” is one tap.

Who makes it

We’re a family in Raleigh. Triangle Tots is published by Danford Enterprises, LLC, our own small company, which also makes the free Tot Toolbox apps. It opened on October 28, 2026. Nobody pays to be in a plan.

How a plan is made

  • Every outing is real. Events are read each morning from the calendars of Wake County Public Libraries, Durham County Library, the towns’ parks departments and local museums. Each one keeps its own time, address and link. The 294 parks, libraries and places to play come from each town’s own pages and map data.
  • Software drafts the day. An AI model picks an outing for each age and writes the day around it. It works only from those calendars and places, and it may not invent an event, a time or an address.
  • Rules check every plan. Before a plan goes up, our own checks make sure the outing suits the age, fits around the nap and is on when the plan says. A plan that fails is made again or left out.
  • It’s checked again each morning. If an event is cancelled or rain would spoil the day, the plan changes and shows what it was before.

When we get it wrong

Calendars change and places close without notice, so check the event’s own page before you go. If a plan sent you somewhere that was closed, moved or not as we said, please tell us, and we’ll fix it.

What it costs

The daily plans, the guides, search and the Sunday email are free and stay free. Family plans, which start from your own front door and fit every child’s naps at once, are how the site pays for itself. What we keep about you is very little.
