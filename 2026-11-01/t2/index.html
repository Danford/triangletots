Arrive 8:20 am, leave by 11:30 am · Cost not listed · Sign-up needed · Outdoors

  • 8:30 am–5 pm Exploration Station: American Bison

The Exploration Station at Yates Mill is a free drop-in outdoor program celebrating National Bison Day, a 2-year-old can learn about the US national mammal with hands-on displays right beside the beautiful mill pond.

  • Registration is required; confirm your spot the day before.
  • Dress in layers, rain is possible today, so bring a light waterproof layer just in case.
  • The mill pond path is lovely; let your child lead the pace and look for ducks while you visit.
