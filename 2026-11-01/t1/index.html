🐾 Hill Ridge Farms

703 Tarboro Rd, Youngsville, NC 27596 · North Raleigh / Wake Forest

Arrive 11:10 am, leave by 2 pm · Tickets at the gate · Outdoors

Hill Ridge Farms has animals, pumpkins and wide open fall festival grounds, a full, unhurried morning for a toddler after the first nap on a mild day. Mid-week crowds are gone by Sunday so the pace is still easy.

  • Pack a snack and water; there is plenty of walking and the child will be hungry mid-morning.
  • Give a five-minute warning before leaving so the transition isn't a surprise.
  • If showers arrive, head to the car early and save the rest of the farm for another day.
