Sarah P. Duke Gardens

Your toddler can toddle along the garden paths, look at plants and visit the Charlotte Brody Discovery Garden. It is a gentle, free late morning that ends well before the afternoon nap.

  • Parking is paid and often fills before 10 am, so leave a few minutes to find a spot.
  • Bring a snack and let them stop and poke at leaves along the way.
  • Head back to the car while they are still happy, and point out one last flower on the way out.
