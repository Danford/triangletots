  • 🌷 NC Museum of Art Museum Park

    Raleigh · 10 am–12 pm

    Map · Free

    Your child wanders trails, lawns and big outdoor art at the NC Museum of Art Museum Park. They can choose which way to go next.

    • Play a finding game: the biggest, the funniest, the shiniest artwork.
    • Bring a snack to eat on a lawn.
    • Let your child lead the way and pick the last stop.

  • 🌿 Occoneechee Mill Village Hike

    Occoneechee Mountain State Natural Area - Eno Mountain Road Access · Chapel Hill / Pittsboro · 12:50 pm–3 pm

    Map · Cost not listed · Sign-up needed

    A ranger leads a hike through the old Occoneechee Mill Village. Much of it is off trail among old brick and rebar. A family needs to sign up.

    • Wear pants and closed-toe shoes, as the ranger asks.
    • Eat lunch before you go and bring a snack.

    Durham · 2 pm–4:15 pm

    Map · Free
