Or swap in

  • Hemlock Bluffs Nature Preserve

    Cary / Apex · 12:30 pm–2:30 pm

    Map · Free

    Your child can walk the trails and boardwalks through the woods at Hemlock Bluffs, taking the stairs and stopping to look at whatever they find.

    • The trails have stairs, so pack a carrier for tired legs.
    • Bring a snack for halfway.

  • Beech Bluff County Park StoryWalk® 2026 (Self-Led), then Beech Bluff County Park

    Beech Bluff County Park · South Wake · 1:30 pm–3:30 pm

    Map · Free · No sign-up needed

    Read the story page by page along the Educational Loop, then play at Beech Bluff County Park. It is free and self-led, with no sign-up.

    • Let your child lead the way to each page.
    • Bring a snack for the playground.

  • NC Botanical Garden

    Chapel Hill / Pittsboro · 12:30 pm–2:30 pm

    Map · Free

    Your child can follow the display garden paths and then head onto the forest trails behind them, stopping wherever they like. Free, and open from 9 am.

    • Bring a snack and eat it on a bench before the forest trails.
    • Let your child pick which path to take at each turn.
    • Say 'one more look' ten minutes before you leave so the goodbye is easy.

  • Lake Benson Park

    South Wake · 6 pm–7:30 pm

    Map

    An afternoon at Lake Benson Park gives your child a playground and greenway to explore after the nap. It is a good way to burn off energy before dinner.

    • Bring a snack and a water bottle.
    • Head home while there is still plenty of daylight so the evening stays calm.

  • JC Raulston Arboretum

    Raleigh · 2:15 pm–4 pm

    Map · Free

    After quiet time, your child can wander NC State's garden at their own pace. It is free, and the arboretum closes at 4:30 pm.

    • Bring a snack and a water bottle.
    • Give your child a job, like finding three different leaves.
