Arrive 11:10 am, leave by 2 pm · Cost not listed · No sign-up needed · Outdoors

  • 10 am–6 pm 📅 Trick-or-Treat at North Hills

Trick-or-Treat at North Hills is a free outdoor Halloween event with dozens of stops across the shopping area, perfect for a toddler in costume who loves exploring and collecting treats on a mild October morning.

  • Dress the child in their costume before you leave home so they are ready to go the moment you arrive.
  • Bring a small bag or bucket for treats and a snack from home for the walk between stops.
  • Give a ten-minute heads-up before leaving so the transition away from the excitement isn't a shock.
