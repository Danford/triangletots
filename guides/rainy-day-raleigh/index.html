Or stay in

  • Kitchen band

    Pots, pans, wooden spoons and a box of rice to shake. March around playing.

  • Tape roads

    Painter's tape roads across the floor for toy cars, with a block tunnel and a parking spot.

  • Puppet show

    Socks or stuffed animals behind the couch put on a show; you get to be the audience.

  • Stack and topple

    Stack cups or blocks as high as they can go, then knock them down and start again.

  • Stickers

    A sheet of big stickers and a piece of paper; peel one corner to start each for little fingers.

  • Shaving-cream tray

    Squirt shaving cream on a baking tray and draw in it with fingers. A drop of food colouring to swirl.
