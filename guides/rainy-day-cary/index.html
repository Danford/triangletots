Or stay in

  • Beanbag toss

    Toss beanbags or rolled socks into a laundry basket; step back after each one that goes in.

  • Book basket

    Spread a few favourite books on the floor and let them choose; read the same one twice if they ask.

  • Songs with hands

    Slow songs with actions: Itsy Bitsy Spider, Twinkle Twinkle, Wheels on the Bus.

  • Homes for the animals

    Give each toy animal a home in a box or basket: the farm on one side, the zoo on the other.

  • Pizza back

    Make a pizza on their back: knead the dough, spread the sauce, sprinkle the cheese. Then swap.

  • Freeze dance

    Put on a song and dance; pause it and everyone freezes.
