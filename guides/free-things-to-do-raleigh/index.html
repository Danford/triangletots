Free gardens and nature

  • 🌳 Gipson Play Plaza at Dix Park

    715 Biggs Dr, Raleigh, NC 27603

    Free; free parking. Big destination playground on the south edge of Dix Park.

  • 🌷 JC Raulston Arboretum

    4415 Beryl Rd, Raleigh, NC 27606

    Free. NC State’s garden; no pets.

  • 🌷 NC Museum of Art Museum Park

    2110 Blue Ridge Rd, Raleigh, NC 27607

    Free. 164 acres of trails, lawns and big outdoor art; the Capital Area Greenway runs through.

  • 🌷 Raleigh Rose Garden

    301 Pogue St, Raleigh, NC 27607

    Free. A small terraced rose garden behind Raleigh Little Theatre, good for a short walk; no public restrooms, parking on Pogue Street.
