Or stay in

  • Ball drop

    A pile of soft balls to roll, chase, and drop into a box or basket.

  • Hide the toy

    Hide a favourite toy while they cover their eyes, then say "warmer" and "colder" as they hunt.

  • Scoop and pour

    A shallow tub of dry oats with cups, spoons and a funnel. A towel underneath catches the spills.

  • Family photos

    Look through photos on your phone and name everyone in them.

  • Dinosaur dig

    Hide toy dinosaurs in a tub of dry rice and dig them out with a paintbrush and spoon.

  • Colour sort

    Sort pom-poms or buttons by colour into a muffin tin, with tongs for an extra challenge.
