Or stay in

  • Colour sort

    Sort pom-poms or buttons by colour into a muffin tin, with tongs for an extra challenge.

  • Cushion course

    Couch cushions on the floor to climb, crawl over and jump onto.

  • Balloon keep-up

    Keep a balloon off the floor with hands, feet and heads. Pick up any popped pieces straight away.

  • Kitchen helper

    Wash vegetables, tear lettuce or stir the batter for a snack together.

  • Big-paper crayons

    Tape a big sheet of paper to the floor or table and draw together with chunky crayons.

  • Toy wash

    A dishpan of soapy water, a washcloth and the plastic toys that need a bath. Towel underneath.
