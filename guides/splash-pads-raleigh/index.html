Splash pads in Raleigh

Splash pad season is over for now: the city runs its free splash pads from April to October, 9 am to 9 pm, weather dependent.

Updated Saturday, November 7, 2026. Rebuilt every morning.

    Dix Park’s page had the waterfall and splash pad off for repairs when we read it on October 27, 2026. Check before you go.

    No lifeguard on duty.

  • Moore Square Splash Pad

    225 E. Martin St, Raleigh, NC 27601

    No lifeguard on duty.

At city pools

These are inside staffed pools and take the pool’s admission. The city says the pools open on Memorial Day weekend, and to check its pool schedule. Ages 1 to 12 are $2 for residents, $3 for non-residents.

  • Biltmore Hills Pool

    701 Crown Crossing Lane, Raleigh, NC 27610

  • Lake Johnson Pool

    5623 Jaguar Park Drive, Raleigh, NC 27606

  • Ridge Road Pool

    1709 Ridge Road, Raleigh, NC 27607

Before you go

  • Children who aren’t potty trained must wear swim diapers.
  • An adult has to stay with children.
  • No pets, food, drink or glass containers.
  • The water is turned off in bad weather.
