    Sarah P. Duke Gardens · Durham · 10:50 am–2 pm

    Map · Free · No sign-up needed

    The chrysanthemum display at Duke Gardens is free, outdoor and beautiful, a slow, colourful walk through the Japanese Garden that suits a toddler this age perfectly.

    • Arrive at 10:50 am for a quieter start before midday crowds build.

  • Mill Open House

    Historic Yates Mill County Park · Raleigh · 1 pm–2 pm

    Map · Cost not listed · Sign-up needed

    The doors of the historic grist mill open and interpreters explain how it works, big wooden gears and moving water that toddlers find impossible to look away from.

    • The outdoor grounds around the mill pond are lovely for a short walk alongside the visit.
    • Leave by 2 pm so you are home ahead of the afternoon nap.

    Map · Cost not listed · Sign-up: check its page

    • The sensory-friendly format means you can arrive and explore at your own pace from 10:50 am.
