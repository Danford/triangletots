Arrive 11 am, leave by 1:30 pm · Free · Outdoors

Free trails and display gardens at NC Botanical Garden give a gentle Sunday morning of plants, seeds and nature discoveries at a pace a two-year-old can set for themselves.

  • Arrive at open when the paths are emptiest.
  • The short forest trails at the back feel like a real adventure for little legs.
