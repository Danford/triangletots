Arrive 11 am, leave by 1:15 pm · Free · Outdoors

Wander the display gardens, then follow the nature trails through the forest behind them. It is a good size for a 4- or 5-year-old to explore.

  • Turn the walk into a hunt for the biggest leaf, the smallest flower and something fuzzy.
  • Bring water and a snack for a trail-side break.
  • Pick a last stop early so leaving feels like part of the plan.
