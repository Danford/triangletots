How it works

  1. Tell us about your familyYour home or ZIP, and each child's age, wake-up, naps and bedtime.
  2. Get a day that fitsA morning and an afternoon outing for any day this week, or a day built around an event you pick.
  3. Keep the ones you likeSave days, get an email if one changes, and see them in Tot Routines on your phone.

Questions

Will I be charged during the trial?

No. You pay nothing for 14 days, and cancelling before then costs nothing. After that it's $10 a month or $100 a year, and you can cancel any time from this page.

Where and for what ages?

Raleigh, Durham, Cary and the rest of Wake and Durham counties, for children from 1 to 5.

How is this different from the free plans?

The free daily plans are the same for every child that age. Family plans start from your home and fit all your children's own times. The free plans and the Sunday email stay free.

What do you do with my family's details?

We keep them on your account to make your plans, and that's all: no ad tracking, and we never sell or share them. The privacy page says exactly what we keep.

Is Tot Routines included?

Yes. Tot Routines is free, and a family plan adds Tot Toolbox Supporter for up to 4 phones, plus your saved days showing up in the app by themselves.
