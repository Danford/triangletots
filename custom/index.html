Questions

How is this different from the free plans?

The free plans are the same for every child that age. Family plans use your home and all your kids' own times. The daily plans, the area choice and the Sunday email stay free.

What do you do with my family's details?

We save them to your account so they're there when you sign in, and use them to make your plans. That's all: no ad tracking, and we never sell or share them. The privacy page says exactly what we keep.

I already pay for Supporter. Do I pay twice?

Family includes Supporter for up to 4 phones, so you won't need both. Email support@tottoolbox.com and we'll sort it out.
