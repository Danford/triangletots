Questions

How is this different from the free plans?

The free plans are the same for every child that age, wherever they live, and you choose a broad area. Family plans will start from your home, with exact drive times, fit all your kids' naps at once, and be built around an event you choose.

Is it worth it with one child?

If you're happy with the free plans, stay on them. Family plans earn their keep when the drive matters, when two children's naps have to fit, or when there's one event you've set your heart on.

Are the free plans staying free?

Yes. The daily plans, the area choice and the Sunday email are free.

Are the events real?

Yes. Every outing comes from a library, parks or museum calendar, with its own link. We never make up an event, a time or an address.

What do you keep if I join the waitlist?

Your email address, which list you joined, the area if you give one, and the date. Leaving is one click, and it deletes the entry. The privacy page has the rest.

Events come from Wake County Public Libraries, Durham County Library, the town parks calendars and local museums. Times and details are copied from each event's own page; check it before you go, as things change.
