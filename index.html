How the plans work

  • Only real events. Every outing comes from a library, parks or museum calendar, with its own time, address and link.
  • Built around sleep. Each day has three plans for the common nap patterns at that age, and you're home before the nap.
  • Checked every morning. If an event is cancelled or rain would spoil the day, the plan changes and shows what it was before.

Going with more than one child?

Family plans will start from your own front door, fit every child's naps at once, and work around the event you want to go to. Join the free waitlist and we'll email you once, when it opens.
