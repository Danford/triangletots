Or swap in

  • 📖 Family Storytime, then Heater Park

    Cary Regional Library · Cary / Apex · 10:20 am–11:50 am

    Map · Free · Sign-up not listed

    Family Storytime at Cary Regional Library at 10:30 am has books, music, rhymes and movement for children 0 to 5. Then Heater Park has a playground to run off the wiggles.

    • Arrive early to pick up free tickets at the Youth Services desk, since space is limited.
    • Bring a snack for the park.
    • Tell your child the playground comes next so storytime stays easy.

  • 📅 Colorful Leaves, then Jack Smith Park

    Hemlock Bluffs Nature Preserve · Cary / Apex · 9:50 am–12:30 pm

    Map · $8.00 resident / $10.00 non-resident · Sign-up needed

    Colorful Leaves is a guided preschool nature session at Hemlock Bluffs where you and your child explore autumn together. Jack Smith Park is a short drive after for playground time. A family needs to sign up and pay a fee.

    • Sign up ahead of time.
    • Wear sturdy shoes for light outdoor exploring.
    • Bring a snack for the playground.

  • 📅 Meet the Keepers

    Sarah P. Duke Gardens · Durham · 12:50 pm–2 pm

    Map · Free · No sign-up needed

    Meet the Keepers at 1:00 pm in the Charlotte Brody Discovery Garden is a chance to meet the keepers of the bees, chickens and edible plants.

    • Eat lunch before you arrive.
    • Ask the keepers one question your child thought up on the way.
    • Give a five-minute heads-up before leaving.
