Or swap in

  • 🌷 NC Botanical Garden

    Chapel Hill / Pittsboro · 10 am–12:30 pm

    Map · Free

    Your child can explore the display gardens, then follow the nature trails through the forest behind them. It is a long, quiet walk with lots to spot.

    • Bring water and a snack for a trailside break.
    • Make it a scavenger hunt: a pinecone, a feather, something yellow.
    • Let them lead on the trails.

  • 🏛️ Museum of Life and Science

    Durham · 10 am–1 pm

    Map · $24 adults, $19 ages 3–15, under 3 free

    Your child can visit the farmyard, ride the train and walk the Dinosaur Trail, with treehouses and a creek in Hideaway Woods.

    • Pick two favorite stops and save the train ride for last.
    • Pack a snack and eat outside between exhibits.

  • 🚂 Model Train Exhibit, then Honeycutt Park

    North Regional Library · North Raleigh / Wake Forest · 1:30 pm–3:30 pm

    Map · Free · Sign-up not listed

    Watch model trains travel through different scenes at North Regional Library, then burn energy at Honeycutt Park.

    • Ask your child to find the tiniest scene on the layout.
    • Bring a snack for the park.

  • 🎉 Frost Fest

    Gipson Play Plaza, Dix Park · Raleigh · 2 pm–4:30 pm

    Map · Cost not listed · Sign-up not listed

    Frost Fest fills the afternoon at Gipson Play Plaza at Dix Park, with a big playground for climbing and sliding.

    • Bring a snack to share.
    • Head home before the light fades and the crowds build.
