🎉 Northeast Regional Library

Arrive 9:05 am, leave by 10:15 am · Free · No sign-up needed

  • 9:15 am–9:45 am 🎉 Halloween Hoot Toddler Storytime
  • Then 🌳 Plummer Park · map · if it’s dry

A Halloween-themed storytime with a trick-or-treat parade, costumes are encouraged and the short program is just right for a 3-year-old. Plummer Park is right next door for a run around after.
