Arrive 9:50 am, leave by 12 pm · Cost not listed · No sign-up needed · Outdoors

  • 10 am–12 pm 📅 Walk Through the Seasons

Walk Through the Seasons is a guided outdoor program at NC Museum of Natural Sciences' Prairie Ridge that leads children through a working prairie and native habitats. On a warm, sunny morning it is a wonderful way to start the day.

  • Wear sunscreen and bring a full water bottle; the whole program is out in the open.
  • Let the child ask the naturalist questions, they love curious little visitors.
  • Head straight to the car at noon so there is time for a proper lunch before nap.
