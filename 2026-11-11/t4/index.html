Or swap in

  • 🌳 Lake Crabtree County Park

    North Raleigh / Wake Forest · 9:30 am–12:30 pm

    Map · Free

    Lake Crabtree County Park has a playground, a garden, nature paths and boating. Your child can walk and explore, then play and snack at a picnic shelter.

    • Bring a snack for the picnic shelter.
    • Walk the paths first, then finish on the playground.
    • Hold hands near the water.

  • 📅 Build a Toy Boat

    Bynum Mill Access at Lower Haw River State Natural Area · Chapel Hill / Pittsboro · 9:50 am–11:30 am

    Map · Free · No sign-up needed

    Your child builds a toy boat from recycled materials at Bynum Mill Access, thinking about balance and buoyancy, then tests it. It is free, and a family doesn't need to sign up.

    • Talk about what floats before the build starts.
    • Snack right after the test.

  • 🏛️ Museum of Life and Science, then Storytime in Hideaway Woods

    Durham · 10 am–12 pm

    Map · Included with museum admission · No sign-up needed

    Visit the Museum of Life and Science outside, with Hideaway Woods treehouses and creek, the farmyard, the train ride and the Dinosaur Trail. End with Storytime in Hideaway Woods at 11:30 am.

    • Do the farmyard and train first.
    • Be at Hideaway Woods by 11:30 am for the story.

  • 🌿 Hike with a Naturalist

    Historic Yates Mill County Park · Raleigh · 9:50 am–11:30 am

    Map · Free · Sign-up needed

    Hike with a Naturalist at Historic Yates Mill County Park to discover the plants and animals that live there. A family needs to sign up.

    • Sign up ahead, since this hike needs registration.
    • Wear shoes and clothes for walking at least a mile on natural surfaces.
    • Bring a snack and a water bottle.
